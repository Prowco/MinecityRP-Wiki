💎 Official Unique Setups Ecosystem

Minecity is a comprehensive, production-grade Minecraft Roleplay server built on Purpur 1.21.11 / Java 21, requiring zero client-side mods. Players connect with standard vanilla Minecraft clients while enjoying 3D animated vehicles, balistic guns, proximity voice chat, cellphones, interactive apartments, and realistic public safety services powered by MinecitySuite and MinecityFixerAddon.

🏙️ Citizen's Roleplay Manual

Everything you need to know about starting your civilian life, building wealth, driving, and surviving in Minecity.

1. Getting Started & City Arrival

When you first connect to Minecity, you spawn at the Los Santos International Terminal as an arriving tourist. You will automatically receive an introductory cinematic tutorial guiding you through the city landmarks.

Interactive Tour

If you want to replay the guided introduction at any time, run /tutorial or /servertutorialplus start welcome.

Your Citizen ID Card (DNI)

Every citizen carries a permanent identification card storing your legal name, registration date, and social security details. Police officers frequently request ID during routine traffic stops:

  • /card show — Presents your identification card to the citizen or officer you are facing.
  • /card view — Opens your own personal ID card in your hands.

2. In-Character Communication & Emotes

Minecity features proximity-based text chat channels and complete body animations:

  • Local Chat (Default): Standard messages reach players within a 15-block radius.
  • In-Character Actions (/me <action>): Describes a physical action your character is doing (e.g. /me hands over the car keys).
  • Situational Surroundings (/do <context>): Establishes facts in your scene (e.g. /do The vehicle's engine is smoking heavily).
  • Out of Character (/ooc <message> or /b): Used for questions or remarks outside the roleplay context.
  • Body Poses (/sit, /lay, /crawl, /bellyflop): Sit on stairs, sofas, chairs, lie down in hospital beds, or crawl under obstacles.

3. Smartphone & Live GPS Navigation

Your smartphone is your ultimate tool for staying connected. Execute /phone to open the custom device GUI.

  • SIM Number: You receive a unique 5-digit cellular phone number (e.g. 49201). Share it with friends to exchange text messages or calls.
  • 911 Dispatch: Instantly calls the police department or emergency medical services with your exact street coordinates.
  • Live GPS Particle Arrows: Type /gps to <destination> (such as Hospital, Police, Dealership, Bank, Mining Quarry). Glowing arrows will guide you along the roads step-by-step. Use /gps stop to cancel.

4. Real Estate: Apartments, Houses & Offices

Powered by MinecitySuite (MCRP-Properties), buying or renting a home has never been easier:

  • Type /apartments (or /rent / /buy) to open the interactive property catalogue.
  • Rental Apartments: Inexpensive weekly or monthly leases. Your apartment automatically locks its doors to strangers. If your lease expires, the interior schematic automatically restores clean for the next tenant.
  • Permanent Houses: Luxury villas, penthouses, and suburban houses that belong to you forever.
  • Roommates: Add trusted friends to your doors using /arm addmember <region> <player>.

5. Vehicles, Dealerships & Helicopter Flight

Minecity includes an extensive collection of custom 3D sports cars, cruisers, motorbikes, helicopters, and planes:

Fixed Steering & Elevation Controls (MinecityFixerAddon)

Steering inversion has been completely fixed! When you steer left, your car turns left; when you steer right, it turns right.
Helicopters: Press SPACEBAR to ascend vertically. Look up/down or press SHIFT to descend smoothly!

  • Purchasing: Buy vehicles at dealerships (via DtlTraders). When purchased, the vehicle is automatically delivered straight into your virtual Garage!
  • Virtual Garage (/qav garage): Spawn your vehicle anywhere in parking zones, or click despawn to safely garage it.
  • Refueling: Pull up to any gas station pump and hold the fuel nozzle to refill your tank.
  • Trunk Storage: Sneak and right-click your vehicle's trunk to store contraband, tools, and luggage.

6. Police Department & Criminal Justice

The legal system in Minecity is enforced by both player officers and automated security:

  • Wanted Stars (0 - 5 ★): Crimes like speeding, illegal firearm possession, or bank robberies instantly raise your wanted level. Your stars are displayed in colored text above your head, in the chat prefix, and on the TAB player list.
  • Wanted Persistence: If you die during a shootout with police, your wanted stars persist when you respawn!
  • Arrest Baton (Jail Stick): Officers must hit suspects twice (2 left-clicks) with the baton to immobilize and handcuff them.
  • Handcuffs: Handcuffed suspects cannot draw weapons or sprint and will be escorted by the officer to the squad car.
  • Prison Sentence: Once booked at the precinct, inmates serve time in the city penitentiary until their sentence expires or bail is posted.

7. Hospital, Diseases & Emergency Medicine

Life in the metropolis carries health risks. MinecitySuite features realistic illnesses and emergency medicine:

  • Contagious Diseases: Contracting the flu, pneumonia, or food poisoning causes coughing, slow movement, and blurred vision.
  • Bone Fractures: Falling from high elevations causes broken leg bones requiring medical splints or crutches.
  • Pharmacy (/pharmacy): Purchase over-the-counter medical supplies such as Antibiotics, Morphine, Bandages, and First Aid Kits.
  • CPR & Defibrillator: When a player is downed or unconscious, on-duty paramedics rush with /paramedic revive <player> to save their life!

8. Realistic Survival: Thirst & Hunger

Your health depends on proper hydration and nutrition:

  • Type /survival status to view your hydration percentage.
  • Running, carrying heavy weapons, and high fever accelerate dehydration.
  • If hydration drops below 20%, you experience fatigue, nausea, and intermittent fainting.
  • Drink bottled water, sodas, and coffee from restaurants or vending machines to maintain optimal stamina.

9. Tamagotchi Pets System

Adopt and raise your loyal animal companion:

  • Run /pet shop to browse puppies, kittens, parrots, and accessories.
  • Use /pet to open the status dashboard: feed your pet treats, bathe it, play fetch, and put it to sleep.
  • Use /pet call to summon your pet to your side or ride shotgun in your sports car!

10. Underworld: Gangs, Weapons & Bank Heists

For those who choose the outlaw path, the city offers high-stakes criminal activities:

  • Illegal Weapons: Black market dealers sell custom 3D firearms (Pistols, AK-47, Shotguns, Snipers) and ammunition. Carrying unconcealed firearms triggers automatic 2-star police alerts!
  • Bank Vault Heists: Assemble a crew to breach the central bank vault. Drilling or cracking the safe triggers a silent 911 dispatch to all active officers and a citywide 5-star wanted level.
  • Loot Escape: Successfully carrying the bags of cash to your hideout secures a massive payout, but escaping the police perimeter requires skilled getaway driving.

🛡️ Admin & Server Owner Guide

Comprehensive technical manual, configuration architectures, LuckPerms permissions hierarchy, and runtime injection protocols.

1. MinecitySuite Architecture & Configuration

MinecitySuite is the monolithic custom roleplay core developed specifically for Minecity Roleplay. It centralizes 6 major gameplay systems into a unified, high-performance Java engine:

File Structure inside plugins/MinecitySuite/
  • config-general.yml: Main language selector, database storage type, debug logger.
  • police-config.yml: Arrest baton hits, jail coordinate vectors, wanted stars decay time, fines.
  • paramedic-config.yml: Emergency call timers, illness infection rates, hospital respawn points.
  • survival-config.yml: Thirst decay curves, sprint dehydration multiplier, item hydration values.
  • pets-config.yml: Pet prices, hunger depletion rates, level progression thresholds.
  • dummy-config.yml: Training dummy AI configurations and testing equipment.

2. MinecityFixerAddon: Runtime Fixer & Enhancer

MinecityFixerAddon is a post-world runtime injector designed to fix bugs and enhance third-party closed-source plugins (such as QualityArmoryVehicles2, GPS, and ForcePack) without modifying their original jars:

  • Vehicle Steering Inversion Patch: Injects into QAV packet listeners to normalize left/right turning controls so vehicles turn naturally in modern 1.21.11 Paper/Purpur.
  • Helicopter Elevation Control: Fixes vertical lift calculations so helicopters ascend with Spacebar and descend with Shift/Pitch.
  • Shop-to-Garage Auto Deposit: Automatically catches vehicle purchases made via DtlTraders and stores them into the virtual Garage instead of dropping buggy physical vehicle items in player inventories.
  • PacketEvents 1.21.11 Packet 66/67 Disconnect Fix: Intercepts unmapped server play packets that previously disconnected players during vehicle interactions.
  • Decorative Vehicle Model Item Generator: Adds /vehicle model get <vehicle_name> allowing staff to spawn physical vehicle models purely for showroom displays.
  • Multi-Language Engine: Full translation dictionary for 9 languages (EN, ES, DE, FR, IT, PT, ZH, KO, RU).

3. Vehicle Showrooms & Dealership Setup

To create an interactive car or motorcycle dealership:

  1. Create an NPC with Citizens (e.g. /npc create Car Dealer).
  2. Link a DtlTraders shop to the NPC offering vehicle vouchers.
  3. Place a decorative vehicle model on display using /vehicle model get <name>.
  4. When players buy the vehicle voucher from the NPC, MinecityFixerAddon intercepts the purchase and immediately registers the car to the player's /qav garage.

5. Testing Dummy System (/ms)

Admins can spawn controllable test dummies to evaluate police batons, arrest mechanics, and paramedic CPR without needing a second human player:

  • /ms adddummy <name> — Spawns a realistic dummy player at your target block.
  • /ms dummycontrol <name> — Forces the dummy to toggle between standing, sitting, and laying in a bed.
  • /ms removedummy <name> — Removes the dummy entity safely.

6. Troubleshooting & Optimization Fixes

Quests Sign Interaction Fix (Purpur 1.21.11)

In modern Minecraft 1.21+, materials like OAK_WALL_SIGN are block-only and throw IllegalArgumentException: ... isn't an item when accessed by older quest listeners. QuestsMinecity.jar has been patched with toBlockItemStack(), cleanly mapping wall signs and wall torches to their valid item forms.

Citizens Clean Shutdown Patch

Citizens-2.0.41-b4129.jar has been patched in AbstractNPC.java to ensure that when /stop is executed, internal hologram sub-NPCs do not attempt to register event listeners while disabled, completely preventing the IllegalPluginAccessException: Plugin attempted to register Spawned{true} while not enabled warning.
